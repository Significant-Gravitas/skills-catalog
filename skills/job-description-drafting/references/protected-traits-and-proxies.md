# Protected traits, proxies, and the job-related rewrite

Never recorded, inferred, or used on the bar (Sofia's boundaries): gender,
race, ethnicity, age (including a graduation year used to estimate it),
religion, nationality or national origin, sexual orientation, gender
identity, disability, health, pregnancy, marital or family status. Nothing is
read off a photo.

## Rewrite table

| Heard | Why it is a problem | Job-related rewrite |
|---|---|---|
| "Digital native" | Age proxy [34] | Name the tool or skill: "ships features in React" |
| "Young, energetic team", "recent graduate", "age 25-35" | ADEA help-wanted terms [45] | Describe the work pace: "a small team shipping weekly" |
| Graduation year cut-off | Age estimate | Cut |
| "Native English speaker" | National-origin proxy | "Writes customer-facing docs in English" |
| "Culture fit" | Works as a class and demographic proxy [79] | A named behaviour ("reviews designs in writing") or cut |
| "Local only", postcode, commute radius | ZIP-code proxy (Illinois HB 3773) [72] | "On-site in Lisbon three days a week" |
| "No kids / can you travel with a family?" | Marital/children questions can evidence intent [74] | "Travels to client sites two weeks a quarter" |
| "Healthy", "no medical issues", "able-bodied" | ADA: no disability questions pre-offer [46][35]; UK s.60 [58] | "Can lift 10 kg, with or without reasonable accommodation" |
| "No employment gaps", "stable history" | Gap patterns are alleged (not ruled) to act as disability proxies in Mobley [108] | Cut; short tenures become an interviewer note, never a disqualifier |
| Photo requested | No photograph before an offer [34] | Cut |
| "Citizens only" | Often a work-authorisation question in disguise | "Authorised to work in <country>"; confirm wording with counsel |

## Also out of bounds for intake research

- No search for health, family, or genetic information about anyone,
  including the benchmark person; GINA treats an internet search likely to
  surface family medical history as a "request" [47].
- Meeting notes from Granola: quote only job-related sentences; never store the
  transcript.
