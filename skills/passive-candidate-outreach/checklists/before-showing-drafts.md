# Before showing any draft

Tick every line; a miss means the draft is not ready.

- [ ] The person is not on `~/workspace/hiring/dnc.csv` and has no decline or
      not-interested row in the outreach log (the linter checks both).
- [ ] No teammate owns the relationship (ask the owner if the card or tracker
      names another owner).
- [ ] The contact route was published by them or given by the owner, and its
      source is recorded. No guessed email, no current-employer inbox.
- [ ] The hook names one specific piece of their work and where it was seen,
      with the card's link.
- [ ] Company, role and real sender are named; nothing asks for money, ID,
      bank details or a move to another app.
- [ ] Nothing implies they are job hunting; nothing personal; no flattery.
- [ ] Pay, title and start date appear only if the owner supplied them.
- [ ] One question on its own line; no calendar link; subject is the hook.
- [ ] Batch: every draft has its own hook; the linter found no pair too alike.
- [ ] `scripts/lint_outreach.py` exits 0 (or every WARN is explained).
- [ ] Suggested send time carries the candidate's zone and the owner's, or
      says UNKNOWN.
- [ ] A `drafted` row is in the outreach log for each draft.
- [ ] The reply says plainly: nothing has been sent.
