# First note (touch 1)

Fill every `<...>`. Delete any line you cannot support from the candidate card.
Default length: under 60 words (default; confirm with the owner, saved as
`outreach_max_words`). Run `scripts/lint_outreach.py` before showing it.

```
Subject: <the hook: the specific piece of work, in 4-8 words>

Hi <first name as they publish it>,

<Hook: one sentence naming the specific work and where you saw it.
 e.g. "I read your post on ... (your blog, March)".>
<Role in one line: "<Company> is hiring <one/a> <role> to <outcome>",
 and why their work fits it.>
<Why it might interest them: the problem, scope, people or stage. One line.>

<One question, on its own line, answerable in a word. No calendar link.>

<Sender full name>, <sender title>, <Company>
```

Send details (shown to the owner, not in the note):

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| To (route) | <published professional route> — source: <where it was published> |
| Suggested send | <weekday> <HH:MM> <candidate IANA zone> / <owner time> <owner zone>, or UNKNOWN |
| Hook source | <URL or document, as on the card> |
| Pay / title / start | only if the owner supplied them; otherwise leave out |
| Log row | <candidate> \| <date> \| <channel> \| touch 1 \| drafted |

Honesty line for the owner: FACT lines come from the card (with its link);
anything else is left out, not softened.
