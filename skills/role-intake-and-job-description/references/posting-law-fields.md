# Posting fields that may be required by location

For each posting location, the fields that **may** be required. Harper never
fills a figure, never says a field is legally required, and never says a
posting is compliant. Each matching field stays `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]` and gets
one line on the approval checklist for the people lead: "may be required for
<location>; confirm". Checked 2026-09-27; laws change. Source numbers refer to
`sources.md`.

| Location | Fields that may be required | Also | Sources |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| California (employers with 15+ employees) | Good-faith pay scale in the posting | No salary-history questions, and no reliance on salary history | [56] |
| Colorado | Pay as a closed range; a general description of benefits; how and when to apply ("open until filled" is not enough) | | [57] |
| Other US states and cities | Salary-history bans exist in several; posting-pay rules not covered by this reference | Ask the people lead which rules apply | [75] |
| New York City, if an AI tool screens applicants | Candidate notice at least 10 business days before the tool is used; public bias-audit summary | Flag only; the tool owner handles it | [52][69] |
| Illinois, if AI is used in hiring decisions | Notice to candidates | ZIP code must not be used as a proxy | [72] |
| Illinois, if AI analyses video interviews | Notice, explanation, consent | Deletion within 30 days of the applicant's request | [55] |
| EU, if AI screens applicants | High-risk system duties apply from 2027-12-02 | Solely automated decisions restricted (GDPR Art. 22) | [53][119][54] |
| UK | No pay field in this reference | No health questions before an offer (s.60) | [58] |
| Anywhere | Accommodation route; candidate AI-use policy per stage | See the lines below | [35][104] |

## Standard lines (fill from the hiring plan; otherwise `[OWNER TO CONFIRM]`)

Accommodation line:
> Tell us about any adjustments you need at any stage of the process: <route>.

Candidate AI-use line, one clause per stage the owner has a policy for. An
example policy published by one employer is "draft yourself, then refine with
AI" for the application and "no AI in live interviews unless told" [104].
> Using AI tools: <stage>: <policy>. <stage>: <policy>.

Pay line when approved:
> Pay: <range> (<basis>, e.g. annual base). Benefits: <summary>. Apply by <date> via <route>.

## Never

- A pay figure, band estimate, or "competitive" in place of an approved range.
- A request for salary history or current pay [56][75].
- "This posting is compliant with ..." or "not required in ...".
