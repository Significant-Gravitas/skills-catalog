# Pre-approval checklist (mirror in TodoWrite)

1. Intake brief, `requirements.csv` and plan read; anchor guide read.
2. Criteria chosen from approved outcomes; each separable and citing a requirement.
3. Four anchors per criterion in observable terms; `not_assessed_rule` filled.
4. Confirm method and one named scorer per criterion.
5. `rubric-v<N>.md` saved from the template with the exact header row.
6. `validate_rubric.py --requirements`: zero ERROR; every WARN fixed or shown.
7. Structure checklist components 8 and 9 marked.
8. Shown to the owner: table, weighting, evidence rules, disallowed signals, warnings.
9. Owner's approval sentence received in chat (not assumed).
10. `approve_rubric.py` run with the quote; `--write-back` run; plan updated.
11. Rubric delivered and linked; checksum reported.
12. If this replaced an approved version: candidates screened on the old one listed for the owner.
