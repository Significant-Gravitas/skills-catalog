# Kickoff checklist (mirror in TodoWrite)

1. Look first: memory, `ls ~/workspace/hiring/`, old plan, pasted or Drive material.
2. One `ask_question` card with only the unanswered questions.
3. `init_role.sh <role-slug>` (exit 2 = update the existing plan).
4. Fill `hiring-plan.md`: owners named or OPEN; policies with who stated them and when; pay OPEN unless approved.
5. `policy_flags.py` with the hiring locations; copy flags; one open question per flag for the people lead.
6. `plan_check.py`: zero ERROR lines.
7. Deliver the plan with `write_workspace_file(source_path=...)` and link it.
8. Store company-level facts in memory (never candidate data).
9. Stage list in `TodoWrite`, stage 1 in progress.
10. First deliverable: JD draft (no approved JD) or rubric (approved JD exists).
11. Self-check from SKILL.md before replying.
