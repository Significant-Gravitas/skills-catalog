# Message shapes

Three to five sentences. Lead with the answer or the request, state the next
step, give a reply date. Every time carries its zone. Times come from the
tracker or the owner, never invented. Sound like the owner if they have
shown you how they write. All of these are drafts until the owner says yes
to that specific message.

## Candidate: loop options

```
Subject: Interview times for <role> at <company>

Hi <first name>, thanks for making time. Here are two options for the
<length> conversations with the team:
- <Day DD Mon>: <HH:MM–HH:MM your zone> (<HH:MM–HH:MM owner zone>) — <what it covers>, with <interviewer>; ...
- <Day DD Mon>: ...
The whole process: <every stage, who they meet, when a decision comes>.
Could you reply by <day, date> with the one that works?
<sender>
```

## Candidate: confirmation (after the booking is made)

Times in both zones, what each conversation is about, who greets them, the
link or address, the accommodation contact, the AI-use policy for interviews
if the owner has one, and when they will hear back.

## Interviewer note

```
<name>, you're on <candidate>'s loop, <Day DD Mon HH:MM owner zone>, owning
<competency>. Kit and packet: <link>. Scorecard due <deadline with zone>,
filed before the debrief on <date>.
```

## Nudge

What is outstanding, since when, and when it is needed:
"Lena, your scorecard for Priya Nair (Monday's system design slot) is still
open. The debrief is Friday 11:00 Lisbon; could you file by 17:00 today so
it's in before anyone talks?"

## Decline

First: the background-check question (`references/fcra-and-verification.md`).
Then brief and plain: the decision in the first two sentences, specific
thanks, no made-up reason, feedback only when the owner hands you real,
approved feedback. Late-stage declines happen by phone: draft talking points
(decision, thanks, one approved reason if any, what happens to their data)
instead of an email.

## Offer follow-up

What is still unresolved, the agreed deadline, and who the candidate can
talk to. No new terms: offers belong to job-offer-and-close-plan.

## Keep-warm

A truthful line on the role's status and when they will hear next. Never
"you're our top candidate" unless the owner says so.

## Holding update (response window)

"Hi <name>, a quick update: the team is still finishing its feedback from
<day>. You'll hear from me by <date> either way." Only dates the owner can keep.
