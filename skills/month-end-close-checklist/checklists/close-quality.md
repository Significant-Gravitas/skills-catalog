# Close quality check (run before sending the close report)

## Discipline

- [ ] Every status came from `close_status.py` (or is labelled hand-tracked if Python is missing).
- [ ] No control is ready for review while a dependency is not started or blocked.
- [ ] The late-source blocker is stated with its owner and expected date; nothing was estimated or reconciled to a feed to work around it.
- [ ] Prior-period closing balances are approved, or the gap is carried as a named open item.
- [ ] Every open item and exception from earlier periods appears with its original period.

## Evidence

- [ ] Each ready control links its evidence (workpaper, review table, aging, brief).
- [ ] Bank reconciliations came from `statement-reconciliation`, not re-done by hand in this skill.
- [ ] Control totals per account match the reconciliation workpapers.
- [ ] Every proposed adjustment has source, reason, preparer and approver, and is marked as a draft.
- [ ] Qualitative-materiality items and payroll-tax matters were routed whatever their size.

## Words

- [ ] The period is called "prepared for review" at most; never "closed", "final" or "approved" unless the owner's recorded reply says so.
- [ ] Checks performed and checks not performed are both listed.
- [ ] Every default (calendar, thresholds) is labelled "default — confirm with the owner".
- [ ] No full account or card numbers; no tax or accounting policy chosen.

## Actions

- [ ] Nothing was posted, locked, backdated, sent or deleted.
- [ ] The binder, if built, is delivered as a file; sending it waits for a yes that names the recipient.
- [ ] Follow-ups or routines were created only after the user said yes to the exact wording.
